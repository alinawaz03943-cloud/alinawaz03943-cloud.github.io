About

I'm a GIS Analyst specializing in geospatial analysis, remote sensing, zoning, land use planning and GIS automation. I hold an MS in Geomatics (GIS and Remote Sensing) from the University of Peshawar, a Bachelor's degree in Geography and a Post-Graduate Diploma in Geomatics.

At Nook Office Solutions I handle zoning and parcel data, georeferencing, digitization, spatial QA/QC, web map research and GIS data extraction. Earlier, at HP Consultant Planners, I worked on master planning and land-use planning projects, and at the Excise and Taxation Department and the Board of Revenue I digitized parcels and cadastral maps.

A large part of my work is making GIS faster. I use Python and AI-assisted development to build tools for CRS correction, geometry validation, zone-change detection, sliver and overlap checks, and a REST spatial-data downloader that exports ArcGIS services to GeoJSON and GeoPackage.

I want to work on geospatial projects where spatial data and careful analysis help planning, environmental management and decisions.
